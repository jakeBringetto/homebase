Overnight HRV

Average overnight HRV (RMSSD, ms) per night, with the 7-day average and your normal range. A 7-day average below the range, or a sudden drop, often follows hard training, illness, poor sleep or alcohol. Read the trend, not single nights (Plews et al. 2013).{% if fallback_band %} Where Garmin has no baseline yet (it needs about 19 nights), the range is your 60-day mean ± 1 SD.{% endif %}

Night-to-night variation

Coefficient of variation of ln HRV over the last 7 nights (needs 4). Studies of endurance athletes (Plews et al. 2012, 2013) found it adds to the 7-day average: a steady or rising average with low variation went with good adaptation, while a falling average with rising variation went with poor adaptation or illness. Compare it with your own usual level, not other people's.
