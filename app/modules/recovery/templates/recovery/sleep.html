Regularity

Sleep Regularity Index for each pair of back-to-back nights: the chance of being in the same state (asleep or awake) at any time on both days, scaled so 100 is identical timing and 0 is chance. In a large UK cohort, people with more regular sleep had lower mortality risk, and regularity predicted it better than duration (Windred et al. 2024). That study measured every minute with a wrist sensor, including naps and waking in the night. This version uses only Garmin's main sleep window, so it reads higher: compare it with your own history, not the study's numbers.
