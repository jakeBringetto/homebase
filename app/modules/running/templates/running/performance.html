Cadence

Steps per minute while moving. The line is the average per {{ chart_unit }}, weighted by run time; dots are single runs. Cadence rises with pace, so compare runs at similar paces (the pace profile below breaks it down by pace). A higher step rate at the same pace means a shorter stride, which lowers hip and knee loading (Heiderscheit et al. 2011).
