Long-run spikes

A run more than 10% longer than your longest run in the previous 30 days. In a 5,200-runner study (Frandsen et al., BJSM 2025) such single-run spikes raised injury risk more than gradual weekly increases did. Points above the line are spikes; the line drops when a long run is more than 30 days old.

Monotony and strain

Monotony is a week's average daily load divided by its standard deviation, rest days included: high means every day looks alike, with no real easy or rest days. Strain is the week's load times its monotony. Foster (1998) found illness followed weeks of high strain, and flagged monotony above {{ mono_high | round(1) }}. Complete Monday–Sunday weeks only.
